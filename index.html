Need to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book an appointment?

Complete the booking form. It opens WhatsApp with your request so our team can confirm with you.

Do you make custom measurements?

Yes. Bespoke garments are made around your measurements and agreed fitting requirements.

Can I pay online?

Yes. Use the Paystack checkout and keep your payment reference. Confirm the amount with the studio before paying.

Can I contact the studio before paying?

Yes. Call 07061401382 or 09012282762, or contact us through WhatsApp.

Does payment automatically confirm an appointment?

No. Online payment does not by itself confirm an appointment. Please wait for confirmation from the studio.

Trust & Transparency

Policies

Contact the studio if you need clarification before placing an order or making a payment.

Privacy Policy

We use details you submit, such as your name, phone number and email, to respond to enquiries, appointment requests, orders and payment questions. Payment processing is handled by Paystack. Do not send card PINs or one-time passwords through this website or WhatsApp. Contact the studio to request correction of details you have provided.

Payment & Refunds

Confirm the price and service with the studio before paying and keep your payment reference. If you believe you paid in error or need help, contact the studio promptly with the reference. Refund requests are reviewed based on the order, work already completed, agreed terms and applicable law.

Terms of Service

Appointment requests are subject to studio confirmation. Before work begins, confirm the design, measurements, fabric, price, fitting arrangements and expected delivery date. Bespoke results depend on agreed specifications. Nothing on this site overrides customer rights under applicable law.
